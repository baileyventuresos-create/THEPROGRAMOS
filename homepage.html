Assessment-driven athlete development · youth through adult

Test. Train. Retest.
Every 28 days.

Every other platform starts with programming. The Program starts with measurement: a standardized 8-test athletic assessment, repeated every 28 days, coach-administered and coach-verified — with 130 movement games prescribed against what the assessment actually shows. No automatic prescriptions from scores. The coach always decides; the system makes sure the decision is based on evidence.

01 · TEST

Measure

Your coach runs the GAA battery — sprint, jump, shuttle, strength-endurance, balance, and a 6-minute walk/run — under standardized protocols. Results are coach-recorded measurements, with the method attached.

02 · TRAIN

Develop

Your coach assigns training from the 130-game movement library — in person, remote, or both — and sets the dose. You record completions; your history builds.

03 · RETEST

Prove it

28 days later, you test again. Baseline, previous, personal best — progress you, your parent, and your coach can all see. Then the cycle repeats.

The GAA battery is a standardized field protocol that has not yet been field-validated. Results are presented as coach-recorded measurements only — never as norms, percentiles, rankings, or diagnoses.
